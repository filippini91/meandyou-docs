When you open a client from your [Clients](/planners/clients) list, you step into the exact same planning dashboard the couple uses themselves — budget, tasks, guest list, website, and everything else — with a few extra shortcuts available only to planners.

  **Messages** and **Tasks** are the two exceptions: clicking them from inside a client's wedding takes you to the workspace-wide [Messages](/planners/messages) or [Tasks](/planners/tasks) page, already filtered to this client, rather than opening a page scoped to just this wedding.
</Note>

## Switching between clients

While you're inside a wedding, a client switcher at the top of the Client interface block lets you jump to a different client without leaving the section you're on — if you're looking at a budget, switching clients takes you straight to the new client's budget too.

## Planner-only shortcuts

A few places in the shared dashboard show extra controls only when a planner workspace member opens them:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Messages: template button">
    The chat composer includes a templates icon that prefills your message from a saved [message template](/planners/templates#messages) — the same shortcut available on the workspace [Messages](/planners/messages) page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Vendors: Add from library">
    On the Overview page's vendors section, the add-vendor button has an extra option to pull a vendor straight from your workspace [rolodex](/planners/vendors) instead of typing one in from scratch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calculator: Add from template">
    Next to the usual "New item" button, planners get an "Add from template" option that inserts an item from your saved [calculator templates](/planners/templates#calculator).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Schedule: import a template">
    An empty schedule shows an "Import template" option to bring in a whole [schedule template](/planners/templates#schedule) group at once; once a day exists, you can also add a single item from a template.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Questionnaire: import a template">
    The questionnaire editor lets you import a whole [questionnaire template](/planners/templates#questionnaire) group, or add one templated question at a time. Remember to save the questionnaire afterward to keep the import.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emailing the couple">
    For something more formal than a chat message, use **New email** from the client's [details page](/planners/client-details) to send a one-off email to the couple.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  All of these shortcuts are also available to the [AI assistant](/planners/ai-assistant) — you can ask it to apply a template or add a vendor from your rolodex instead of clicking through the UI yourself.
</Tip>
