If a colleague adds you to their me&you professional workspace, you'll get an invitation link by email. This page covers what happens when you open it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    The link points to an invitation that's tied to your email address and a specific workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in if needed">
    If you're not signed in to me&you yet, you'll be sent to sign in or create an account first. You'll return to the same invitation link afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept">
    Once you're signed in, the invitation is accepted automatically and you're added to the workspace with the role your colleague chose for you (admin or member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    You're redirected to the workspace's [Home](/planners/home) page a moment later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The invitation isn't found">
    The link may be mistyped or the invitation may have been withdrawn. Ask whoever invited you to send a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation has expired">
    Invitations don't stay valid forever. Ask your colleague to send a fresh invite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This invitation isn't for your account">
    Invitations are tied to a specific email address. Make sure you're signed in with the same email the invitation was sent to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation was already used">
    If you've already accepted it before, opening the link again just takes you straight back into the workspace — nothing's broken.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
