The Activity page is a feed of what's changed across every client wedding in your workspace — a quick way to catch up without checking each wedding individually.

## The feed

Each entry shows who made a change, what changed (for example, a task was completed, a vendor was added, or the wedding date changed), which client it belongs to, and when it happened. Where it makes sense, clicking an entry takes you straight to the relevant place — like the task that was updated or the client's messages.

## The New badge

Entries you haven't seen yet are marked **New**. The badge clears the next time you visit the page after viewing it, so you always have a clear sense of what's happened since your last check-in.

## Searching and filtering

Use search to find activity by keyword, or the filter button to narrow the feed down by client, type of change, or date range.
