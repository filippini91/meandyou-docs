The Clients page lists every wedding you work on. From here you open a specific client to work inside their wedding, or add a brand-new one.

## Browsing your clients

Each client is shown as a card with the wedding name, the couple and any other members on it, a note about unread messages, and upcoming tasks. A small highlighted dot marks clients that need your attention — an unread message or a task due soon.

Use the search box to find a client by wedding name, and the sort control to order the list by date added, name, or by putting the clients that need attention first. A **Show archived** switch reveals clients that have been archived.

## Adding a new client

Click **New client** to open the client form:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wedding name and couple">
    Give the wedding a name and enter both partners' names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the couple">
    Add one or more email addresses. Each person gets an invitation to the wedding; add a row for each additional person you want to invite right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose starting templates">
    Pick which [templates](/planners/templates) to start the client with for tasks, calculator, questionnaire, and schedule — or leave them empty. Your [client defaults](/planners/workspace-settings#client-defaults) are pre-selected here, so you only need to change them if this client needs something different.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who's billed">
    Decide how this client is billed:

    - **The client pays** — after you create the client, they'll receive invites and a 30-day trial begins. They can add payment details at any point during the trial.
    - **You cover it (license)** — the client starts with no trial, and one of your workspace license credits is used instead. This option is only available if you have a license credit available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    The client wedding, invites, and starting templates are all set up for you right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting the couple

When someone you've invited opens their invitation, what they see depends on how they were invited:

- If you invited them from the **New client** dialog, they get a standard wedding invitation and simply accept it to join their own wedding.
- If you invite them later from inside the wedding's own settings, and me&you recognizes their email as belonging to a planning professional, it can offer them a choice between **joining privately** (only they get access, and the wedding won't show up in any professional workspace) and **joining as a professional** (the wedding is added to their own workspace, and their colleagues get access too).

<Warning>
  If a wedding is already linked to another planning workspace and someone accepts an invite as a professional, it moves to their workspace and the previous workspace loses access. This warning is shown clearly on the invitation screen before anyone confirms.
</Warning>

See [Working inside a client's wedding](/planners/working-inside-a-clients-wedding) for what happens once you're inside, and [Client details](/planners/client-details) for managing an existing client.
