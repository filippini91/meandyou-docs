Setting up a me&you professional workspace takes you through a short wizard that collects your business details, has you accept the planner terms, and creates your workspace. This page walks through each step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get started">
    You can start the wizard without signing in yet — the first few steps don't require an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account type and country">
    Choose whether you're operating as an **individual** or a **business**, and select your country of operation. If you're VAT registered, switch that on and enter your VAT ID — me&you validates EU VAT numbers automatically and can prefill your business name and address from the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business details">
    Enter your name or business name, registration number (if you selected business), and address. This is also where you're asked to sign in or create an account — you can't continue past this step anonymously.

    <Note>
      If you already have an affiliate account with me&you, you'll be offered the option to copy your existing business details into your new planner workspace instead of typing them again.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Planner terms">
    Read and accept the terms that apply to professionals using me&you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledgements">
    Confirm you understand four things: how client data is handled, how billing and invoicing work for your account, that you're responsible for keeping your business and tax details current, and how pricing works when you resell me&you to your own clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about you">
    Add at least one link where clients can find you (your website, Instagram, etc.) and a short description of what you do. Finishing this step creates your workspace and takes you to your new [Home](/planners/home) page.

    <Tip>
      We also set you up with a free test wedding inside your new workspace, fully unlocked with no trial limits, so you can explore and try out every feature of me&you before adding a real client.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your progress is saved automatically as you go, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off later on the same device.
</Tip>
