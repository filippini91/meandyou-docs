Home is the landing page of your professional workspace. It gives you a quick way to start a conversation with the AI assistant, jump to any section, and see what needs your attention across every client.

## Chat composer

At the top of Home is a text box where you can type a question or request straight to the [AI assistant](/planners/ai-assistant) — for example, asking for a daily or weekly summary across your clients. A few suggested prompts are shown as quick-tap chips. This box only appears if the AI assistant is turned on for your workspace.

## Quick navigation

A row of shortcuts links straight to Messages, Tasks, Activity, Clients, Templates, and Vendors. Messages, Tasks, and Activity show a count and a small dot when there's something new.

## Updates summary

A single line sums up what's waiting for you: unread messages, unseen activity, and tasks due soon, each linking straight to the relevant section.

## Three update columns

Below the summary, three columns show the five most recent items in each area:

- **Messages** — your latest unread conversations.
- **Activity** — recent changes across your clients' weddings you haven't seen yet.
- **Tasks** — tasks due within the next 7 days across all clients. Clicking one opens it for editing right from Home.

## Recently visited clients

If you've opened any client weddings recently, up to three show up here as quick-access chips. Click a chip to jump straight into that client's wedding, or use the chevron to jump directly to a specific section of their plan (like their guest list or budget) without opening the full client page first.
