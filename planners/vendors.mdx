Your workspace keeps its own rolodex of vendors — venues, caterers, photographers, and anyone else you work with across multiple weddings — so you don't have to re-enter the same contact every time a new client needs one.

## Adding a vendor

Click **New vendor** and fill in a name, a category (like venue, catering, or photography), and one or more contact methods (website, email, phone, Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn). If the category is **Venue**, you can also set a location on the map.

## Editing and removing a vendor

Click any vendor card to edit it, or use its menu to delete it. Deleting a vendor from your rolodex only removes it from your own list — it doesn't touch any client wedding you've already added it to.

## Using a vendor in a client's wedding

When you're [working inside a client's wedding](/planners/working-inside-a-clients-wedding), the vendors section offers an **Add from library** option alongside the usual add-vendor button. Picking a vendor from your rolodex copies its details into that client's wedding as a starting point — from there, it's a normal vendor entry on their wedding that you (or they) can edit independently.
