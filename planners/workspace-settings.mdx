Workspace settings is where you manage everything about your professional workspace itself, rather than a specific client's wedding. It's a single scrolling page, organized into sections.

## General settings

Your business name and profile picture, plus the primary contact email clients see on emails from you. Changing the contact email requires confirming the change from both the old and new address.

## Members

See everyone on your team, invite new members by email (as an admin or a member), change an existing member's role, or remove them. Pending invitations that haven't been accepted yet are listed here too.

<Note>
  Only owners and admins can manage members, and nobody but the workspace owner can hold the owner role or remove it from themselves.
</Note>

## Billing

Set your **default billing method** for new clients — either the client starts their own 30-day trial, or you cover them using one of your workspace license credits with no trial. You can buy more license credits here, and review your payment method and past receipts.

## AI assistant

Turn the [AI assistant](/planners/ai-assistant) on or off for the workspace, decide whether members (not just owners/admins) can chat with it, and choose which model it uses. From here you can also buy AI credits, connect your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, and manage access tokens for using the assistant through Claude or ChatGPT — see [How the AI assistant works](/planners/ai-assistant) for the full picture.

## Client defaults

Choose which task, calculator, questionnaire, and schedule templates are applied automatically whenever you create a new client. See [Templates](/planners/templates) for what each template type contains.

## Notifications

A shortcut into your personal account notification preferences, since those apply across your whole me&you account, not just this workspace.

## Privacy

Download a complete export of your workspace's data as a ZIP file.

## Danger zone

Visible only to the workspace owner. Deleting the workspace is permanent: you'll need to type a confirmation phrase first. Deleting a workspace doesn't delete any client's wedding — it just removes the connection between that wedding and your workspace.

## Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work inside client weddings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Invite, remove, or re-role members | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage AI settings, BYOK key, and MCP tokens | Yes | Yes | No |
| Buy licenses or AI credits | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change client defaults and general settings | Yes | Yes | No |
| Delete the workspace | Yes | No | No |
