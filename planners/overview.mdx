me&you's professional workspace is a separate space from a couple's own wedding plan: it's where you manage every client wedding you work on, your team, your reusable templates, and your vendor contacts in one place. This page maps out the workspace so you know where to go for each task.

## What the workspace is

When a couple plans their own wedding, they use me&you at `/plan`. When you work with them as a professional, you do it from your workspace at `/planners` instead — a separate dashboard that lists all of your clients and lets you jump into any of their weddings to help directly.

<Note>
  Joining or creating a workspace doesn't take anything away from the couple. They keep full ownership of their wedding; you're added as a collaborator with planner-level access.
</Note>

## Roles in a workspace

Every workspace has one or more members with one of three roles:

- **Owner** — the person who created the workspace. Can do everything, including deleting the workspace and transferring billing.
- **Admin** — can manage clients, members, templates, vendors, and most settings, but can't delete the workspace.
- **Member** — can work inside client weddings and use the shared tools, but can't manage team members, billing, or workspace-wide settings.

See [Workspace settings](/planners/workspace-settings) for the full breakdown of what each role can do.

## Working inside a client's wedding

When you open a specific client from the Clients page, you step into their actual wedding plan — the same budget, tasks, guest list, and website tools they use themselves, plus a few planner-only shortcuts. See [Working inside a client's wedding](/planners/working-inside-a-clients-wedding) for how that works.
