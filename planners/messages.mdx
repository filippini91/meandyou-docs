The workspace Messages page puts every client conversation in one place, so you don't have to open each wedding individually to check in with a couple.

## The conversation list

On the left, you'll see one row per client wedding: the wedding name, a preview of the last message, when it was sent, and a dot if there's anything unread. Use the search box to filter by wedding name.

## The conversation

Select a conversation to open the full chat with that couple on the right. It's the exact same conversation the couple sees on their side — anything you send here reaches them directly, and anything they send shows up here.

## Sending a message

Type in the composer at the bottom and press **Send** (or Enter). You can attach files with the paperclip icon.

## Using message templates

Click the templates icon in the composer to open your saved [message templates](/planners/templates#messages). Picking one drops its text straight into the composer, ready to send as-is or edit first. This is the fastest way to send a common update (like a weekly check-in) without retyping it for every client.
