Opening a client from your [Clients](/planners/clients) list takes you to their details page — a summary of that one wedding, separate from the full planning tools covered in [Working inside a client's wedding](/planners/working-inside-a-clients-wedding).

## What's on this page

- **Users** — everyone with access to the wedding, plus any invitations still pending.
- **Status** — whether the client is active or archived, and their trial or billing status.
- **Default assignee** — pick one of your workspace colleagues as the primary person for this wedding. Whenever the couple assigns a task to your business as a whole instead of a specific person, it lands with this colleague behind the scenes, so it's worth setting on every active client.
- **Created** and **Wedding date** — the wedding date can be set or changed right from here.
- **Tasks** — this client's task list, with the same search, filter, sort, and template tools as the main [Tasks](/planners/tasks) page, already scoped to this one client.

## Actions available from this page

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="New email">
    Send a one-off email directly to the couple (and anyone else on the wedding who isn't already a member of your workspace). Useful for something more formal than a quick chat message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages">
    Jump straight into your ongoing conversation with this client in [Messages](/planners/messages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="View as client">
    Open the wedding exactly as the couple sees it on their own `/plan` dashboard — useful for checking how something looks from their side.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mute notifications">
    Turn off notifications for this specific client without affecting any others.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Detach client">
    Remove this client from your workspace. You'll be asked to confirm first — the couple keeps their wedding and everything in it, your workspace just loses access to manage it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Detaching a client

If you stop working with a client, you can detach their wedding from your workspace. The couple keeps their wedding and every bit of their data — detaching only removes it from your client list and ends your workspace's access to it. Open the three-dot menu in the top right of this page and choose **Detach client**, or ask the [AI assistant](/planners/ai-assistant) to do it for you — either way you'll be asked to confirm before anything happens.
